The Spectre Monotile

Discovered in 2023 by Smith, Myers, Kaplan, and Goodman-Strauss, the Spectre is an aperiodic monotile (an "einstein") that tiles the plane infinitely without ever repeating a periodic pattern, using strictly non-reflected copies.

  • Drag & Drop: Drag tiles from the left palette onto the board, or move existing tiles.
  • Rotate Tile: Use the 'R' key or Mouse Wheel while dragging/hovering to rotate tiles in 30° steps.
  • Pan & Zoom: Click and drag the empty canvas background to pan. Scroll mouse wheel on background to zoom in/out.
  • Snapping: Tiles automatically snap to adjacent matching vertices and edges when dropped nearby.
