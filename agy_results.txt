## Theorem Proved

  `no_minimal_phason (p1 p2 : Patch) : ¬ IsMinimalPhason p1 p2`

  Proof strategy:
    1. Assume IsMinimalPhason p1 p2 (same boundary, no shared tile).
    2. Gauss-Bonnet gives wordCurvature (patchBoundary p1) = 12.
    3. Classical case split on ContainsLock (patchBoundary p1):
       - Lock present → lock_forces_shared_tile → patchIntersect p1 p2
         → contradicts ¬ patchIntersect p1 p2.
       - No lock → lock_free_curvature_bound → wordCurvature ≤ 2
         → contradicts 12 = wordCurvature (omega).

## Design Notes

  - `Patch` is `opaque` rather than a concrete structure because the
    uniqueness argument only needs the boundary-word and intersection API.
    Concrete geometry lives in Monotile.lean / Locks.lean.

  - `ContainsLock` enumerates 5 lock patterns drawn directly from
    `isLockedState` (2 short locks) and `completesLongLock` (3 long locks)
    in TransferMatrixBatches.lean.

  - The curvature units are 30°-steps (AllowedStep.toStep values).
    12 step-units = 360°.  The transfer matrix batches prove cycle
    curvature ≤ 60° = 2 step-units for punctured (lock-free) walks.

  - `by_cases` was replaced with `rcases Classical.em ...` because
    `ContainsLock` involves existential quantifiers (∃ i, ...) and
    thus is not computationally decidable.
